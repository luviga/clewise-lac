# Honduras: what is published

Checked 21 November 2026. Source ids refer to `parts/sources_HON.csv`.

**Current NDC: NDC 3.0 (HON-01).** Spanish, 72 pages, by SERNA, submitted on 21 January 2026 (UNFCCC folder 2026-01). It covers 2021 to 2035 and sets a single-year target for 2035: net emissions of all sectors, LULUCF included, 12,847.2 Gg CO2e below a BAU of 65,616.6 Gg, that is 19.6 percent, to at most 52,769 Gg (p.23, p.25). For 2030 it keeps 8,564.8 Gg below a BAU of 50,381.3 Gg, about 17 percent. Every sector target is conditional on international climate finance (p.13, p.17). The BAU is a linear projection of the 2011-2020 inventory (p.23), and in it energy emissions fall from 9,161 Gg in 2030 to 8,963 Gg in 2035 (p.24). The split by sector is energy 2,519.1 and 3,778.6 Gg in 2030 and 2035 and agriculture 4,534.3 and 6,801.5 Gg; IPPU, waste and LULUCF take 503.8 and 755.7 Gg each (p.25). The energy and transport measures are 70 percent renewable generation by 2030, held to 2035, and a 3 and 5 point rise in the electricity share of final use by 2030 and 2035 (p.26). In transport it sets 5 percent biofuels by 2030, an electric fleet of 3 percent by 2030 and 7.5 percent by 2035, and hybrids at 20 percent of light vehicles and 15 percent of freight vehicles by 2035. Household firewood use falls 39 percent against 2012 by 2030 (p.27). The ICTU appendix has only its cover page in the submitted file (p.68), and the technical annex with the measure sheets is not published.

**Earlier NDCs.**
- HON-02 is the second update of April 2025 (UNFCCC folder 2025-09). It keeps 16 percent below a 2030 BAU of 28,945 Gg without LULUCF and adds 214.3 Gg for LULUCF, for 4,845.5 Gg against a net BAU of 50,381.3 Gg (p.3, p.4). The brief's lead record (`..._21f1`) holds this document, not NDC 3.0.
- HON-03 is the first update of May 2021. It sets 16 percent below BAU in 2030, 1.3 million ha in restoration and 39 percent less household firewood.
- HON-04 is the INDC of September 2015, the first NDC from 2016. It sets 15 percent below BAU in 2030.

**Long-term strategy: none submitted.** NDC 3.0 says the LTS, the Estrategia Nacional de Descarbonizacion y Resiliencia Climatica (ENDRCH), is being consolidated and was used only as guidance, not as a submission (HON-01 p.14, p.15). The BTR1 says its update began in 2024 and its projections were still being validated (HON-05 p.563 to p.565). No published ENDRCH document was found (`parts/sources_pending_HON.csv`).

**Transparency and inventory.** HON-05 is the BTR1 of December 2024, with the inventory for 1990 to 2022. In it, 2022 emissions are 21,795 Gg CO2e without LULUCF, of which energy is 10,880 Gg. Its projection section describes the ENDRCH work and prints no projections. HON-06 and HON-07 are the older inventory report (2016-2020) and the BUR2, both dated February 2024.

**New since 1 January 2025:** the second NDC update (HON-02, April 2025), NDC 3.0 (HON-01, January 2026) and the approved generation expansion plan PIEG 2026-2035 (HON-08, September 2026).

**Sector plans.** NDC 3.0 cites two plans for quantified targets.
- The PIEG 2026-2035 (HON-08) for the 70 percent renewable target. Its approved scenario reaches 76 percent in 2030 and 71 percent in 2035 and builds 1,796 MW of renewables and 1,285 MW of thermal plant, including 250 MW of gas combined cycle in 2029.
- The national electric mobility strategy (ENME) for the fleet targets. It could not be fetched.
