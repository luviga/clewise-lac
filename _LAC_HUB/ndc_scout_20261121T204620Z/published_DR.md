# Dominican Republic: what is published

Checked 21 November 2026. Source ids refer to `parts/sources_DR.csv`.

**Current NDC: NDC 3.0 RD-2025 (DR-01).** Spanish, 222 pages with annexes, titled 2025 and uploaded to the UNFCCC registry in June 2026 (Climate Policy Radar record dated 9 June 2026). It replaces the 2020 update (DR-02, December 2020) and the first NDC, which is the August 2015 INDC (DR-03, English unofficial translation, archived). Headline: a 28 percent cut in GHG emissions against the trend (BAU) scenario in 2030 (14,732.1 kt CO2eq, of which 8.5 points unconditional and 19.5 conditional) and 32 percent in 2035 (18,349.32 kt CO2eq), both against the NDC's own BAU of 51,949.62 and 57,292.65 kt CO2eq, base year 2022 (DR-01 p.14, p.62). The press figures of 28 and 32 percent are confirmed. The NDC lists 26 policies and measures; the energy ones are 1,429 MW of new gas combined cycle plants and 1,529 MW of new solar, wind and hydro by 2030, a distribution loss programme, and a small modular reactor of up to 300 MW by 2035 (p.148 to p.151).

**Long-term strategy: none submitted.** The UNFCCC long-term strategies portal could not be read by script (bot check), and Climate Policy Radar lists no long-term strategy for the country. NDC 3.0 names a long-term low-emission development strategy (p.120) without a date or link, and no copy was found (`parts/sources_pending_DR.csv`). The 2050 goal appears only as an aspiration to carbon neutrality (DR-02 p.26; DR-04 p.111). The national long-term plan the NDC cites is the National Development Strategy 2030, Law 1-12 of 2012 (DR-06), marked not submitted to the UNFCCC. Its horizon is 2030, and its climate indicator is 2.8 t CO2 per capita in 2030.

**Transparency and inventory.** First Biennial Transparency Report combined with the Fourth National Communication (DR-04, 30 December 2025). It reports NDC 2020 progress and without-measures projections to 2035 by sector, and it cites a Ministry of Energy and Mines target of 30 percent renewable generation by 2030. National Inventory Document 1998-2022 (DR-05, 2025). It is the inventory behind the NDC's 2022 figure of 45,327 kt CO2eq (DR-01 p.128), and it holds no projections or targets.

**New since 1 January 2025:** NDC 3.0 (DR-01), BTR1/NC4 (DR-04) and the NID (DR-05).

**Sector plans:** the NDC cites the National Energy Plan 2025-2038 for its demand and planning assumptions, not for a stated target. It could not be fetched and is listed as pending. No other sector plan is cited for a quantified energy or transport target.
