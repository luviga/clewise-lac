# Costa Rica: what is published

**Current NDC: NDC 3.0, "Contribucion Nacionalmente Determinada 2025-2035" (CR-01).** MINAE and the Climate Change Directorate wrote it, dated October 2025. The registry record gives submission on 14 November 2025. It is in Spanish and includes the Second Adaptation Communication. It sets two absolute, economy-wide, net targets covering all sectors of the 1990-2021 inventory, both with AR5 GWPs:

- a 2030 cap of 11380 Gg CO2e;
- a 2025-2035 budget of 132700 Gg CO2e.

It lists 40 mitigation contributions with milestones for 2027, 2030 and 2035: 16 in energy and transport, 4 in industrial processes, 7 in agriculture, 4 in land use, 3 in waste, 3 for oceans and 3 for just transition. The document says most of them are conditional (p.149). The plan was modelled with CLEW-CR-3.0, which the document says extends OSeMOSYS-CR.

**Earlier NDCs (all superseded):**

- The 2020 update, as the full version (CR-04) and the headline-target note (CR-05). It set a 2030 cap of 9.11 Mt CO2e and a 2021-2030 budget of 106.53 Mt, with SAR GWPs.
- The 2015 INDC, which became the first NDC (CR-08). It set a 2030 cap of 9374000 t CO2e. The UNFCCC Spanish original returned a bot-check page, so CR-08 is the English text from a third-party (V20) copy.

**Current long-term strategy: the National Decarbonization Plan 2018-2050 (CR-02 English, CR-03 Spanish).** It is on the UNFCCC long-term strategies portal with the date 12 December 2019. Its goal is net-zero emissions by 2050, organised in 10 axes with goals for 2022, 2030, 2035 and 2050. No newer long-term strategy was found. NDC 3.0 refers to the Plan as the long-term frame (p.19, p.143) and says its action plans need updating to AR5 and the new inventory categories (p.143).

**Transparency:**

- BTR1 (CR-06): submitted at the end of 2024, 424 pages, in Spanish. It reports progress on the NDC 2020 contributions. It prints no emission projections; the hub should confirm whether it formally uses the flexibility provision for this.
- The newest inventory document (CR-07): the 1990-2021 NID (2024). It reports 16155.4 Gg CO2e excluding LULUCF and 12632.5 Gg net for 2021.

**Sector plans:** NDC 3.0 cites the ICE Generation Expansion Plan 2022-2040 as the vehicle for its 95 percent renewable target (p.36, p.46). That plan could not be found online and is listed as pending.

**New since 1 January 2025:** NDC 3.0 only. BTR1 and the NID are dated 2024.
