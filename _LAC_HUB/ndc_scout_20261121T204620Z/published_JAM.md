# Jamaica: what is published

Checked 21 November 2026. Source ids refer to `parts/sources_JAM.csv`.

**Current NDC: NDC 3.0 (JAM-01).** English, 23 pages, from the Climate Change Branch of the Ministry of Economic Growth and Job Creation. The PDF was created on 19 September 2025 and sits in the UNFCCC 2025-09 folder. It sets single-year targets for 2030 and 2035 against a 2012 base year, with BAU equivalents. Unconditionally, energy and cement emissions (forestry excluded) fall 16.3 percent below 2012 by 2030 (19.55 percent below BAU) and 16.9 percent by 2035 (25.9 percent below BAU). With forestry the cuts are 26.3 and 27.1 percent below 2012. Conditional on support, the figures rise to 19.6 and 28.3 percent without forestry and 30.6 and 41.7 percent with it (p.2, p.4, p.5). Gases: CO2, CH4, N2O and HFCs, AR6 values. In absolute terms, energy emissions are 5.8 Mt in 2030 and 5.7 Mt in 2035 (unconditional), and 5.5 and 4.9 Mt (conditional) (p.21). The quantified energy elements are a renewable share of net generation of 50 percent in 2030 and 63 percent in 2035, and system losses of 17.7 and 16.45 percent of generation (p.14). Cement enters the NDC for the first time, with a 40 kt cut in 2035 (p.2). Mangrove, seagrass and forest measures are listed with numbers but not counted in the target (p.7, p.16, p.17).

**Earlier NDCs.** JAM-03 is the June 2020 update (NDC 2.0). It cut energy and land-use change emissions 25.4 percent (unconditional) and 28.5 percent (conditional) below a 2030 BAU of 7.2 MtCO2e. JAM-04 is the 2015 INDC, which set energy-only cuts of 7.8 and 10 percent below BAU in 2030.

**Long-term strategy: LT-LEDS (JAM-02).** The 2050 Long-term Emission Reduction and Climate Resilient Strategy carries a cover date of November 2023. NDC 3.0 says it was approved in 2024 (JAM-01 p.3). The brief's lead gives 29 August 2025 for its UNFCCC submission. The UNFCCC page returned a bot-check, so the Climate Policy Radar copy is used; that PDF was last modified on 27 August 2025. Its headline goal is economy-wide emissions 73 percent below 2020 by 2050, with indicative net zero by 2060 (p.13). Milestone tables set renewable electricity at 27, 50, 65, 90, 95 and 100 percent for 2025 to 2050, falling T&D losses, an HFO phase-out by 2035, electric vehicles at 67 percent of the passenger fleet by 2050, and building, industry, forestry and agriculture milestones (p.14, p.15).

**Transparency and inventory.** No first Biennial Transparency Report or National Inventory Document of Jamaica was found. NDC 3.0 says the BTR "will be prepared" (p.18). The newest inventory documents are the Third National Communication (JAM-06, 2018) and the first BUR (JAM-05), both covering 2006 to 2012. The TNC prints a LEAP baseline projection to 2055 (p.346).

**New since 1 January 2025:** NDC 3.0 (JAM-01) and the UNFCCC submission of the LTS (JAM-02).

**Sector plans.** NDC 3.0 cites the 2022 Integrated Resource Plan (IRP-2, JAM-07, final August 2023) as a data source. Its 63.4 percent renewable share for 2035 matches the NDC figure, and it sets a 50 percent renewable portfolio standard for 2030 with 344 MW of wind, 1,143 MW of solar and 493 MW of storage by 2041. The superseded IRP-1 (JAM-08) is kept for the earlier 30 percent target. The National Energy Policy 2009-2030, the Electric Vehicle Policy (2023) and the Biofuels Policy are cited by the LTS without a number taken from them, so they were not fetched.
