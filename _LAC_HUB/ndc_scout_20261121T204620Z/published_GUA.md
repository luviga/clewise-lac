# Guatemala: what is published

Checked 21 November 2026. Source ids refer to `parts/sources_GUA.csv`.

**Current NDC: NDC 3.0 (GUA-01).** Spanish, 136 pages, published by MARN and dated 2026. The UNFCCC file sits in the 2026-11 folder, so it was filed this month. It covers 2026 to 2035 and sets a single-year, economy-wide target: net GHG emissions 23.7 percent below the national trend (BAU) scenario in 2035. That is 57,763.7 kt CO2eq against a BAU of 75,738.0 kt, starting from the 2022 inventory value of 64,382.5 kt (p.28, p.131). The document labels the target neither conditional nor unconditional. It covers CO2, CH4, N2O and HFC with AR5 values. Forests deliver 88.4 percent of the 2035 reduction (15,895 kt), through net zero deforestation by 2035. Energy and transport deliver 1,184.9 kt, the HFC phase-down 894.4 kt, and agriculture and waste nothing quantified (p.27). The energy and transport measures are at least 80 percent renewable electricity and at least 1,000 MW of new renewable capacity by 2035, E10 from 2026, 10,000 more electric and 20,000 more hybrid vehicles than in 2025, bike lanes, the Aerometro cable car, and 25 percent of the Transmetro diesel buses made electric (p.40 to p.48).

**Earlier NDCs.**
- GUA-02 is the 2021 update (NDC 2.0), submitted in 2022. It set 11.2 percent unconditional and 22.6 percent conditional cuts against BAU in 2030, with 80 percent clean generation by 2030.
- GUA-03 is the INDC of 29 September 2015, counted as the first NDC from 2017. It set the same percentages against a 2005-based BAU and 80 percent renewable generation by 2030.

**Long-term strategy: ENDBE (GUA-04).** The Estrategia Nacional de Desarrollo con Bajas Emisiones dates from 2018. The BTR1 (GUA-05 p.260) calls it the long-term strategy. The UNFCCC portal copy could not be fetched because the portal returned a bot-check page, so the FAOLEX copy is used. The strategy offers 43 options and no binding target. With all of them, emissions fall from 203 to 83 Mt CO2e in 2050, 59 percent below BAU (p.58). NDC 3.0 says the ENDBE is being updated and that a PANCC with a 2050 horizon is in preparation (p.22, p.78). Neither document is published yet.

**Transparency and inventory.** The first Biennial Transparency Report (GUA-05) is dated March 2025. Its Section I is the newest National Inventory Document, with net emissions of 64,382.5 kt CO2eq in 2022. Section II tracks NDC 2.0: 2022 emissions were 2.7 percent above the with-measures path, and the start of E10 slipped to January 2026 (p.252, p.254). GUA-06 is the older inventory report for 1990 to 2018 (August 2023). Neither inventory prints emission projections.

**New since 1 January 2025:** NDC 3.0 (GUA-01) and BTR1 with its NID (GUA-05).

**Sector plans:** the LTS cites the Plan Nacional de Energia 2017-2032 (GUA-07) for 64 percent renewable generation in 2032 (GUA-04 p.24). That plan also sets a 29.2 percent cut in energy emissions against BAU in 2032. NDC 3.0 mentions the Politica Energetica 2013-2027 and the national efficiency policy 2023-2050, but gives no number from either, so neither was fetched.
