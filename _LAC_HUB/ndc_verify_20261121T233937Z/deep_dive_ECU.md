# Ecuador: what a deeper study would still need

**Where the evidence stands.** The NDC in force is the second NDC 2026-2035 (ECU-01, February 2025). It states few numbers a model could take: its sector lines of action are qualitative and only 8 NDC rows reach `scenario_values.csv` (with the Plan Maestro de Electricidad, ECU-07, which it cites). The long-term values come from the PLANMICC 2024-2070 (ECU-03), which the NDC names (ECU-01 p.10, p.33) but which was not submitted to the UNFCCC as a long-term strategy; 83 LTS rows are marked `plan named by the NDC`. Of 114 values checked, 85 reached 3 of 3 and 29 reached 2 of 3, all for want of a second place; there are no conflicts. The NDC (ECU-01 p.66) names OSeMOSYS as the projection tool and an AFD interview (ECU-25, lead) says a CLG team built the PLANMICC model, so the two government documents and the CLG model are not independent.

**What is missing.**
1. The OSeMOSYS-Ecuador model and its futures (MAATE or the CLG team), to read the PLANMICC series by sector and year; the PLANMICC prints most of them only as labelled points on charts.
2. A definition of the SNI shares in the PLANMICC table (ECU-03 p.104): whether self-generation and isolated systems are in the denominator. The model renewable split is computed over all plant types; the non-conventional share in NDCNEW 2035 is 0.196 against a phase I target of about 0.24, and hydro 0.608 against about 0.64.
3. The ENEM (2021, IDB), still not found, to define the fleet shares behind the PLANMICC transport hypotheses (ECU-03 p.106, p.107), and vehicle stock data to compare them with the model's transport output shares.
4. The background note 4 of the World Bank CCDR (ELENA inputs, named on ECU-22 p.88): ELENA is the only independent model family found (ECU-22, ECU-23) and uses a different BAU.
5. A check of the PLANMICC phase targets that are periods (2024-2035, 2036-2050, 2051-2070) and were read at the end year of each phase.
6. The 2035 printed values "8.800" and "10.600" kt of the NDC were read with the dot as a thousands separator, consistent with the 7 and 8 percent shares; a ministry confirmation would close this.
