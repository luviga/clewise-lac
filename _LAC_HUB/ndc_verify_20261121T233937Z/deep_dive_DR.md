# Dominican Republic: what a deeper study would still need

**Where the evidence stands.** The NDC in force is NDC 3.0 (DR-01, 2025). Its energy contribution is written as measures with capacities and emission cuts (new gas combined cycles of 1,429 MW, 30 solar parks of 1,267.15 MW, 5 wind parks of 250 MW, a small modular reactor, distribution loss reduction), not as a renewable share. Of 66 values checked, 35 reached 3 of 3, 28 reached 2 of 3 (18 with no second place, 10 where the blind reading was spoiled by a hub error in the blind file) and 3 are conflicts (measure-level reductions that the blind reader read as kilometres or passengers). No long-term strategy is submitted: NDC 3.0 names one (DR-01 p.120), the DDP 2025 chapter (DR-25 p.2, a CLG lead) says the draft awaits launch, and the only long-term value loadable now comes from END 2030.

**What is missing.**
1. The LT-LEDS itself, once launched, to replace the CLG documentation (DR-24) and DDP chapter (DR-25) as the source of every LTS value.
2. The full Plan Energetico Nacional 2025-2038. The CNE bulletin (DR-26) gives only scenario totals for 2038 and USD 5,400 million to reach 30 percent renewables by 2030. The compendia would give the expansion plan by year and say whether the 25 and 30 percent targets (DR-02 p.101, DR-04 p.174) count energy or capacity and whether large hydro counts.
3. A start year and starting capacity for each NDC 3.0 capacity measure, so that the added capacity can be set against the model's installed capacity (the model has 9,421 MW of solar PV in NDCNEW 2035, far above any measure total, which suggests the stage controller shares, not the measures, drive it).
4. The statistical BAU of NDC 3.0 (regressions by gas, annex p.188 to p.189) against the OSeMOSYS-RD Tendencial of DR-24 and against the model BAU: the three come from different methods.
5. The World Bank EPM input book (DR-22 p.18 onward) for plant costs and limits, an independent check of the model's inputs.
6. A re-run of the blind reading of the ten BAU rows by gas (DR-P002 to DR-P015) with the gas named, to bring them from 2 of 3 to 3 of 3.
