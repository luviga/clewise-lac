# Jamaica: what a deeper study would still need

**Where the evidence stands.** The NDC in force is NDC 3.0 (JAM-01, September 2025), which cites the 2022 Integrated Resource Plan (JAM-07); the long-term strategy is the 2050 LT-LEDS (JAM-02, submitted August 2025; the UNFCCC file fetched in this job is byte-identical to the copy used). The NDC sets renewables at 50 and 63 percent of net generation in 2030 and 2035 and system losses of 17.7 and 16.45 percent; the LTS sets 27, 50, 65, 90, 95 and 100 percent renewables for 2025 to 2050, T&D losses falling to 8 or 11 percent, and electric fleet shares. The model has 0.391 renewables in NDCNEW 2030 and 0.750 in 2035 and 2050, and 0.990 in LTSNEW 2050. Of 133 values checked, 109 reached 3 of 3, 18 reached 2 of 3 and 6 are conflicts: two IRP retirement totals that do not match the plan's own figures (a sum the plan never prints, and a total of a different quantity), the 2050 loss target printed as 8 percent in one table and 11 percent in another, a bus target with no year, and a transport value the blind reader did not report.

**What is missing.**
1. The 2021 Vivid Economics and World Bank LTS recommendations with their LEAP tables (JAM-02 p.24): they are the numbers behind the LTS and no public copy was found.
2. A ruling from the Climate Change Division on the 2050 T&D loss target (Table 1 at 11 percent against Table 4 and the text at 8 percent).
3. Confirmation of who did the LTS cost-benefit analysis named in JAM-01 p.22; the UNFCCC case study that names CLG, UWI and CIRED gave a bot check and is unconfirmed. The jam-lts site (CLG) holds a cost-benefit analysis marked preliminary.
4. Grid flows for the losses: the model reports technology outputs only, so a loss share needs the input-activity ratios of `ELE_TRANS` and `ELE_DIST`.
5. The IRP-2 retirements by plant and year (131.5 MW of turbines and 40 MW of diesel in 2027; 60, 124 and 65.5 MW of IPP diesel in 2024, 2026 and 2033, with 2032 on p.62), to compare with the model's fuel oil and diesel capacity.
6. Jamaica's BTR1 and NID, still not found; the newest inventory used is 2006 to 2012.
7. Vehicle stock data to compare the 8, 12 and 67 percent fleet shares with the model's transport output shares.
