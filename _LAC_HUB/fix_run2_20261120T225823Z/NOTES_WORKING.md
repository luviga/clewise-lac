### How the B1 compiler applies config rows by year (read in the Costa Rica runner, lines 2420-2620 and 4096-4240; the other five runners follow the same layout)
- Order: the transport block (Mode_Shift, Tech_Adoption, Distance_Levers, Occupancy_Rate, T_Elasticity) runs first, per scenario, on the stable data of that scenario; then for every scenario the Electrical sheet rows of `All` and of that scenario; then SmartGrid; then Efficiency and EmissionsActivityRatio.
- Electrical rows are generic on (Tech_Set, Parameter). Known points = Exact_Years/Exact_Values plus Milestone_Years/Milestone_Value. `linear_interpolation_time_series`: 0 before the first known year, linear between known years (np.interp), flat after the last known year (Fix_Last). `Interpolate_Escalate` with Fix_Last first repeats the last exact value up to the first milestone year. `Fix_Indicated` uses y_ini plus the first milestone value.
- Built-in NO with method Write: values multiplied by Security_Multiplier, rounded to 4 decimals, and appended as a new set for every year (if A2 already holds that set, the data file gets a duplicate and glpsol rejects it).
- Built-in YES with method Overwrite: the existing values of that tech and parameter are replaced as one slice (assumes one contiguous block, one mode and one time slice). Costa Rica and Honduras only round them; Dominican Republic, Ecuador, Guatemala and Jamaica also multiply by Security_Multiplier. `intact` in Exact_Years keeps the existing values up to y_ini and interpolates to the milestones.
- Year-column sheets (Efficiency, SmartGrid, EmissionsActivityRatio) with Method Exact write every year of the row (a history-only row is not possible). Dominican Republic and Ecuador round Exact emission-factor rows to 4 decimals.
- Transport rows have no year points: logistic (R2021, R2050, L, C, M) or linear (y_ini, v_2030 and later fixed milestone columns), 0 up to y_ini. They cannot be rewritten as year points in their own sheets. Plan for D2a: for every NDCNEW and LTSNEW transport key that differs from BAU in 2018 to 2024 (TotalAnnualMaxCapacity and TotalTechnologyAnnualActivityLowerLimit of TRN or TR techs and groups, `history_diff_before.csv`), add an Electrical Overwrite row with every year explicit: the BAU compiled value to 2024 and the own 4ba69d6 compiled value of that scenario from 2025, divided by the multiplier that the B1 of that country applies to Overwrite rows.
