# d3_ef: external sources for the 39 BAU history emission factors

Retrieved 2026-11-21 (UTC). Unit throughout: Mt of gas per PJ of the technology's activity (power plants: PJ electricity output; PROCRU: PJ crude processed). A factor per TJ of fuel (kg/TJ) becomes Mt per PJ of fuel by x 1e-6; times PJ fuel per PJ output gives the model unit. Unit check: Honduras PPFOI CO2 2019 model 0.171817 = 77,400 kg/TJ x 1e-6 x 2.2199 PJ fuel per PJ output, consistent with the brief's example. Verdict: matches = within 1 percent; differs = both values and the relative difference (model/source - 1); no source = nothing found for that year (the IPCC default and range in the last section is the check for an ASSUMPTION).

## Findings by country

Honduras (23 rows). Every model row equals an IPCC/inventory factor per TJ of fuel times one fuel-per-output ratio per tech and year: the ratios CH4/CO2, N2O/CO2 are exactly the inventory defaults, and CO2e = CO2 + 28 CH4 + 265 N2O to 7e-16 (PPBIM: 28 CH4 + 265 N2O, biogenic CO2 excluded). So the per-TJ part matches the inventory for all years. The fuel-per-output ratio has a published source only for 2019: BEN 2019 Cuadro 5 efficiencies (Bagazo 0.15, GLP 0.38, Diesel 0.35, Bunker 0.46, Coque 0.31; PDF p.58, printed p.31; Cuadro 4 on the same page gives 317.48 kWh/t bagasse, 409.55 kWh/bbl GLP, 573.85 kWh/bbl diesel, 781.90 kWh/bbl bunker, 2,450.32 kWh/t coke). Result 2019: PPGTGLP matches (+0.78 percent); PPFOI +2.1 percent, PPGTDSL and PPICEDSL -3.7 percent, PPCOK +11.9 percent, PPBIM -9.1 percent (model vs source). Other years: no source. The BEN 2018 and 2020-2024 give fuel inputs to "Centrales electricas" in kBEP (for example BEN 2024 Cuadro 7, PDF p.141-142) but generation by fuel only as charts or whole-percent shares (BEN 2023 Figura 29 PDF p.66: fuel oil 32 percent of 11,080 GWh in 2022, 40 percent of 11,606 GWh in 2023; BEN 2024 Figura 25 PDF p.73: térmica 48 percent), and print no kBEP to TJ factor; DGEM 2019 report (p.17) gives 18.1103 kWh/gal bunker and 13.8181 kWh/gal diesel for 2019 only; CEPAL SICA 2024 Cuadro 55 (printed p.73) gives 2023-2024 generation by technology, mixing bunker and diesel ("Motor diesel - derivados de petróleo"). Note: the inventory's "carbon coque" uses other bituminous coal values (94,600 / 1 / 1.5); the IPCC petroleum coke default is 97,500 / 3 / 0.6.

Dominican Republic (6 rows, CO2e). Model values are CO2 only: PPCOA = 0.0946 x heat rate (other bituminous coal, e.g. 2018 0.0946 x 3.76 = 0.355696), NG rows = 0.0561 x heat rate, PPICEFOI = 0.0774 x heat rate. The inventory uses sub-bituminous coal (96,100 kg/TJ) and AR5 CO2e. Source value (form 1) = NID fuel TJ x inventory CO2e factor / (CNE gross generation by fuel, Tabla 2 including Quisqueya 1, GWh x 3.6). Results 2018-2022: coal differs every year (-3.6 to +7.9 percent; the model's 2019 and 2020 heat rates 3.48 and 3.16 equal the source's 2018 and 2019 values 3.467 and 3.160, which suggests a one-year shift); natural gas matches in 2020 (+0.37 percent) and in 2022 when the SENI row of the 2022 balance is used (+0.06 percent; inventory total incl. isolated systems gives -4.7 percent), differs in 2018, 2019, 2021; fuel oil differs (-17.9 to -32.1 percent) and diesel is not comparable, because the inventory fuel includes autoproducers and isolated systems while CNE generation is SENI only. 2023-2024: no source (inventory ends 2022).

Ecuador (10 rows). PROCRU 2022 (form 1): 1,245.5 kt CO2 / 325.734 PJ processed = 0.0038237 Mt/PJ, model 0.0037528 (-1.9 percent); 1,248.9 kt CO2eq gives 0.0038341, model 0.0036340 (-5.2 percent). The model's PROCRU CO2e is below its CO2, which cannot be right with positive CH4 and N2O. CH4 and N2O are printed as 0.0 kt, so only a bound (< 1.5e-7 Mt/PJ) is available; both model values are inside it. 2021, 2023, 2024: no source. Power plants (PPCRU, PPDSL, PPFOI, PPNGS, PPRDSL, PPRNGS): no source for the fuel-per-output ratio. ARCONEL 2024 gives fuel use by fuel 2015-2024 (Tabla 55 physical units, Tabla 56 kTEP, PDF p.61) and generation by technology (Tabla 53: MCI 7,156.96, turbovapor 2,115.35, turbogas 1,724.26 GWh in 2024) but not generation by fuel. Implied ratios: PPCRU 3.935, PPDSL/PPRDSL 2.0815, PPFOI 2.9105, PPNGS/PPRNGS 5.0098 PJ fuel per PJ output (model CO2e / inventory CO2e per TJ).

Crude processed: BCE SerieCifrasPetroleras.xlsx sheet Petroleo row 51 "MATERIA PRIMA PROCESADA EN REFINERIAS (Miles de barriles), TOTAL NACIONAL", sum of 2022 months (columns GA:GL) 56,368.158 kbbl (2021 53,469.83; 2023 53,375.657; 2024 45,468.793); 5.778689 GJ/bbl = IPCC crude NCV 42.3 TJ/Gg (V2 Ch1 Table 1.2) / UNSD 7.32 bbl/t (earlier job GEN-002, GEN-001).
