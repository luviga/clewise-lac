## 1. Judgement calls

1. **D13 scope.** D13 applies to power plants. Nine rows outside the power plants also carry 31.356 and were left as they are: Dominican Republic `IMP_ELE`; Guatemala `DIST_FIR`, `DIST_BIM`, `DIST_BGS`, `DIST_NGS`, `DIST_FLEXFUEL`; Honduras `IMP_ELE`, `STOELE`; Jamaica `STOELE` (`logs/d13_not_plants.csv`). With them excluded, the counts equal F16 exactly: Costa Rica 10, Dominican Republic 29, Guatemala 15, Honduras 20, Jamaica 12 at 31.356 and 3 at 31.4, Ecuador 8 at 31.54.
2. **D7a token.** Only the factor 0.98 inside formula cells of the F10 rows became 0.999 (353 cells in 26 rows). Plain-value cells in the same rows (for example Dominican Republic `PPWNDON` and `PPPVT` 2018 to 2023, written as numbers) were not changed: whether those numbers were made with 0.98 cannot be read from the workbook.
3. **D12 hydro classes.** "Medium (< 450 MW)" hydro (`PPHDAMAM`, `PPHDAMPM`, `PPHRORAM`, `PPHRORPM`) is taken as 50 to 450 MW and gets the 50-year life of ARCONEL-004/24 column "Hidraulica > 50MW"; "Small (<50 MW)" gets 40 years (5 to 50 MW). `PPHD` and `PPIHD` are placed in the 0.5 to 5 MW column (33 years); if `PPIHD` were below 0.5 MW the regulation gives 20 years. Hydro takes the civil-works life (the asset group that defines the plant), not the electromechanical life (35, 33 and 30 years).
4. **D12 engines and turbines.** The oil, crude, LPG and biogas plants are taken as internal-combustion engines (ARCERNNR 2021 reference engine plant, 25 years); `PPNGS` and `PPRNGS` as industrial gas turbines (ARCONEL-004/24, 20 years). If they are engines, 25 years would apply.
5. **D12 `PRONGS`.** The life (25 years) is the contract exploitation period of the Ley de Hidrocarburos, written under an ASSUMPTION with the US class life of 14 years as the low end; the kit and PLANMICC carry 1. A `CapacityToActivityUnit` row of 1 (the A2 default) was added only to keep the row-pair layout.
6. **D12 disagreements between sources (for context).** Hydro civil works: ARCONEL 50, CELEC EP accounts 70, kit 60, PLANMICC 75, NREL ATB 100. Engines: ARCONEL depreciation lives 15, 14 and 6 by engine speed against 25 in the cost-of-service report and CELEC. Gas turbines: ARCONEL 20 (jet 6), CELEC 25, kit, PLANMICC and ATB 30. Geothermal: kit 20, national evaluation period 25, PLANMICC and ATB 30. Grid: national 45 and 35 against kit and PLANMICC 60. ARCONEL evaluation periods were not used as lives (ARCONEL-006/24 p.56 says they are not lives).
7. **D6 2018 ratio.** No downloaded pre-tax gasoline A series covers 2018 (the weekly ANP producer price includes federal taxes), so 2018 takes the 2019 ratio (1.4875), the nearest year with both pre-tax prices, as D6 says. Mean ratio 2018-2024: 1.432409. The Q4 2024 gasoline price (3.88 R$/L less 0.89 R$/L of federal taxes) is printed only as chart labels in ANP bulletin 24, p.15, read on the rendered page.
8. **D3 comparability.** Dominican Republic diesel (`PPCCTDSL`): the inventory's gas/diesel oil use in 1.A.1.a.i includes autoproducers and isolated systems while CNE generation is SENI only, which gives absurd factors (0.78 to 23.8 Mt/PJ); treated as no source, ASSUMPTION written. Dominican natural gas 2022: the NID total gives 0.0977 (differs, written); the SENI-only balance row would give 0.0931 (match); the NID total was used for every year for one consistent method. Honduras 2019: the BEN 2019 efficiencies are printed to two decimals (1 to 3 percent uncertainty); every Honduras row-year that differs by more than that was written. Dominican and Ecuador B1 round Exact rows to four decimals (decided as cosmetic).
9. **D8 split and non-hydro lines.** The sourced hydro PJ (AMM 2025, 19.704670 PJ, held in 2026) is split between `PPHDAM` and `PPHROR` in the 2024 overwrite proportion 40.31 : 2.84. Every Guatemala technology has a plateau overwrite line in 2025 and 2026, so the new hydro shares left those years below 100 percent (Stage 2 hard test T1 failed in 5 country-years, worst sum 0.921). The 12 non-hydro plateau lines of each scenario-year were scaled by one factor so the year sums to 100 percent (their mutual proportions unchanged; factors in `logs/ds1_gua_hydro.csv`). T1 then passed.
10. **D2e rows.** New rows were appended after the last row of the Electrical (and Jamaica SmartGrid) sheet, with Security_Multiplier 1, so B1 writes the values as given (rounded to four decimals). Rounding moves some policy values from 2025 by at most 0.00005 (for example Jamaica `T5DSLTRO` 0.0742758 becomes 0.0743).
11. **Basis column.** `proposals.csv` carries a `basis` column. Rows whose value is a unit conversion (D13, D1, D2f), a value fixed by the decision text (D-ECU2 a and d: 0 and 999,999), or the D2 alignment rule (BAU values to 2024, the scenario's own values from 2025) have no source id; Gate A checks source ids for every row of basis `source` and every id cited anywhere.
