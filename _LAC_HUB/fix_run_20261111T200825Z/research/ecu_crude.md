## Annual history

### How ECUF-01 is laid out
- One sheet, "Petróleo". Monthly data only (no annual totals in the file). Row 2 holds the year label in the first column of each 12-column block, row 3 holds month labels (ene ... dic). Values are hardcoded numbers (not formulas), in thousand barrels (miles de barriles), thousand USD, or USD per barrel as labelled in column B.
- Year blocks: 2018 = EE:EP, 2019 = EQ:FB, 2020 = FC:FN, 2021 = FO:FZ, 2022 = GA:GL, 2023 = GM:GX, 2024 = GY:HJ, 2025 = HK:HV, 2026 = HW:IH, of which only HW:ID (ene to ago) are filled. 2026 is PARTIAL: January to August 2026 (8 months).
- I summed the monthly values of each row for each calendar year and multiplied by 1,000 to get barrels. Export price = sum of row 96 (export revenue, thousand USD) / sum of row 94 (export volume, thousand bbl), i.e. a volume-weighted annual average. For comparison I also give the simple mean of the 12 monthly prices in row 95.
- Checks done: row 5 = row 9 + row 18 exactly in every year; row 37 = row 38 + row 41 + row 42 exactly; row 37 = row 94 exactly.
- Row labels used (column B): r5 "TOTAL NACIONAL" under "PRODUCCIÓN DE PETRÓLEO CRUDO (Miles de barriles)"; r9 "EMPRESAS PÚBLICAS"; r10 "EP Petroecuador (2)"; r11 "Petroamazonas EP(3)(4)(5)(6)"; r12 "Operadora Río Napo (Sacha)(4)"; r17 "- Crudo recuperado Amazonía Viva (7)"; r18 "COMPAÑÍAS PRIVADAS"; r21 "TOTAL NACIONAL" under "PRODUCCIÓN DE PETRÓLEO CRUDO FISCALIZADA"; r37 "TOTAL NACIONAL" under "EXPORTACIONES DE PETRÓLEO CRUDO (Miles de barriles)"; r38 "EMPRESAS PÚBLICAS"; r41 "COMPAÑÍAS PRIVADAS*"; r42 "MINISTERIO DE AMBIENTE Y ENERGÍA"; r44 "TOTAL NACIONAL" under "CRUDO RECIBIDO EN REFINERÍAS Y CONSUMO EN OLEODUCTO (Miles de barriles)"; r51 "TOTAL NACIONAL" under "MATERIA PRIMA PROCESADA EN REFINERÍAS (Miles de barriles)"; r94 "TOTAL EXPORTACIONES DE PETRÓLEO (miles de barriles)"; r95 "Precio (USD por barril) (19)"; r96 "Ingreso por exportaciones de petróleo (miles de USD)".

## Notes

- Units: ECUF-01 volumes are thousand barrels (miles de barriles) per month; revenues thousand USD; prices USD per barrel. ECUF-02 uses barrels and thousand barrels as labelled per table; gas is in barrels of oil equivalent (BOE, "barriles equivalentes de petróleo"); no conversion factor for gas to BOE is stated. ECUF-03 to ECUF-09 use million barrels (MMb), and the MEF/MDEP tables are explicitly "MMb fiscalizados".
- Field production vs fiscalized production: ECUF-01 gives both (r5 and r21). ECUF-06 footnote 89 (PDF p.51) and ECUF-08 footnote 44 (PDF p.46) define fiscalized production as crude actually delivered to the State by operators (public and private) after field own-use, operating losses and the contractors' contractual share; it is the basis for oil revenue in the State budget and differs from gross production. BCE ECUF-03 projects both.
- Refinery load definition (ECUF-06 footnote 92, ECUF-08 footnote 45): crude volume (MMb fiscalized) actually entering domestic refineries for processing; excludes exports, residue and inventory changes. This matches ECUF-01 r51 more closely than r44.
- API: Oriente crude above 20 °API, sulfur above 1%, via SOTE; Napo crude below 20 °API, sulfur above 1%, via OCP (ECUF-01 notes 11 and 12, rows 180-181). 2024 measured averages: SOTE 23.5 °API, OCP 18.6 °API (ECUF-02 PDF p.23). SOTE capacity 360,000 bpd; OCP capacity 450,000 bpd, pumping 17-21 °API crude (ECUF-02 PDF p.23 notes a and b). ECUF-02 PDF p.22 (printed p.21) describes the SOTE as built in 1972 as a 497.7 km line "para un crudo de 30º API" (design crude), and PDF p.9 (printed p.8) has the phrase "más del 80% de la producción petrolera ecuatoriana de 30º API" without further detail; neither is a measured API of current exports. ECUF-04 PDF p.53 gives SOTE capacity 360,000 bpd, 390,000 bpd with drag-reducing chemical. Since 1 Dec 2024 the State administers the OCP (ECUF-01 note 12).
- No density in kg/m3 and no heat content (calorific value) for crude is stated in any of the documents read.
- EP Petroecuador share: about 79% of national crude production in 2025 (127.44 of 160.83 million bbl, ECUF-05 Tabla 3), so ECUF-04 projections (P2) cannot be used as national totals without adding private-company production.
- The BCE (P1) and EP Petroecuador (P2) paths point in different directions: BCE projects national field production rising from 168.3 (2026) to 174.7 million bbl (2030), while Petroecuador projects its own crude falling from 134.96 (2026) to 105.67 million bbl (2029), with exploratory prospects adding 18.77 million bbl in 2029. The documents do not reconcile the two. ECUF-06 PDF p.78 says its medium-term fiscalized production was revised down by 2.4 million bbl versus April 2026, "reflejando una senda de recuperación gradual para las compañías privadas y una menor contribución por parte de las empresas públicas".
